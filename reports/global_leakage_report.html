Source-context ablation

Method. Same calibrated histogram-gradient-boosting recipe and published 60/20/20 split. 'region_included' reproduces the champion pipeline; 'features_only' drops the one-hot region context; 'region_only' learns from region alone. Structural missingness remains available to both feature-bearing variants, so this analysis does not isolate or rule out source identification through missingness. Published validation reference: macro ROC-AUC 0.6781, macro Brier 0.1418.
