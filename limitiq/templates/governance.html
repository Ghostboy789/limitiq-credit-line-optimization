Monitoring

Track schema and range failures, missingness, source mix, score drift, calibration, source-level rank ordering, actions and overrides. Review material deterioration before automated use.

Human override

Record actor, timestamp, original result, override, specific rationale and second-level approval for material exceptions. Review downstream outcomes.

Rollback

Disable automatic increases, retain no-change, freeze and manual-review routing, then restore the prior checksum-verified artifact.
